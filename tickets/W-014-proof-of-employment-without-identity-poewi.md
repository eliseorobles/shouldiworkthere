# W-014 - Proof of Employment Without Identity (PoEWI)

**Phase:** Phase 4 - Voice  
**Depends on:** W-004  
**Status:** DONE

## Scope

Separated verifier and publisher roles: attestation issues a scoped, expiring, single-use credential bound to an employer and relationship claim; publisher redeems it atomically without receiving identity; duplicate prevention stays verifier-side; per-submission capability prevents cross-post linkage.

## Acceptance criteria

Publisher database contains no identity mapping; redemption is atomic and single-use; forged, expired, and replayed credentials fail; separation is documented and testable.
