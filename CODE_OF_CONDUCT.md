# Community code of conduct

We welcome people of every background and experience level. Be respectful, constructive, and
specific. Critique ideas and code, assume good faith, and make room for disagreement.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation, and publishing
someone's private information are unacceptable. Do not use issues or discussions to identify
workplace contributors or publish allegations about individual people. Use synthetic examples.

This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained
for this project. Report concerns privately to **privacy@shouldiworkthere.com**, with the subject
“Community conduct”. Share only the information needed to investigate.

Eliseo Robles is responsible for enforcement. Depending on severity and context, enforcement may
include a request to edit content, removal of content, a warning, or temporary/permanent exclusion.
Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical. Reporters and affected participants may request
reconsideration through the same contact. The project currently has one maintainer and does not
claim an independent appeals body.
